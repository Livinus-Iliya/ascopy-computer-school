Who We Are

Ascopy Computer School Rimau is a computer training and digital skills centre located in Rimau, Kajuru LGA, Kaduna State.

We provide practical computer education for beginners, students, business owners, professionals and anyone who wants to improve their digital skills.

Our training focuses on practical knowledge that students can apply in school, business, employment and everyday life.
